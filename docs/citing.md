# Citing DIMS

The DIMS Dashboard is introduced in:

> Miao, G.Q., Weiss, M., Ni, Y., & Han, J. (2026) DIMS: A No-Code Dashboard Linking Multimodal
> Dynamic Signals to Communication Practices. *Communication Methods & Measures,* 1-17.
> [Online Access](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19312458.2026.2740465) 

> Miao, G.Q., Trujillo J., Bulls L. S., Thornton M. A., Dale R., & Pouw W. (2025). DIMS Dashboard
> for Exploring Dynamic Interactions and Multimodal Signals. In A. Ruggeri, D. Barner, C. Walker,
> & N. Bramley (Eds.), *Proceedings of the 47th Annual Conference of the Cognitive Science Society*
> (pp. 1023-1030). San Francisco, CA: Cognitive Science Society.
> [Online Access](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/8gg7474j)

The code lives at [github.com/dims-network](https://github.com/dims-network).
