DIMS Dashboard Builder

Turn your recordings into a working dashboard — no code.

1 · Your study

Who may see this data? Turns on guards that stop data reaching a git repository by accident: a pre-commit hook, a pre-push hook and a CI check. Choose this if you are not certain. Assets are committed with the study and a GitHub Pages workflow is included.

Dashboard details

2 · Sessions & files

Drop your files here. The builder works out what each one is — .mp4 video, .csv measurements, *_transcript.json, .eaf from ELAN — and which session it belongs to, from its name. A CSV with several measurement columns is split into one per measure automatically.

Two ready-made sessions that ship with the builder — the fastest way to see a finished dashboard before you have data of your own.

More than one camera per session?

Some studies film the same session from several angles. Name the angles and say how the files are named, and the dashboard gets a camera selector. Leave this closed if each session has one video.

3 · Align video & data

When a session's video and measurements have different lengths, the dashboard shows dead space. You can trim the video to a window you pick, or pad the measurements with zeros at either end. Your original files are never modified — the edits are applied to the copies inside the study.

One picture of who is coupled with whom, following the playhead: measures are nodes, coherence between them are edges. Needs cross-wavelet on, for the pairs you want as edges.

Switching this on also switches on the chance level (100 surrogates), because without it no edge can be told from coincidence — an unrelated pair scores about 0.25, not 0.
Groups and placement (optional)

If your measures divide into groups — two people, two conditions — name them here. One line each: Label, pattern, colour. The pattern is optional: leave it out and say below which measure goes in which group. Given, it is a regular expression matched against the measure's name.
